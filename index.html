About

From the counter to the browser

I studied Computer Programming at Seneca, and now I build websites for businesses and commercial clients.

I've spent five years at a Domino's store in Toronto, and today I run it. That job taught me what customers look for before they call, what makes them leave, and how a small business actually runs day to day.

I use that when I build a site. The pages answer the questions customers ask, load fast on a phone, and make it easy to order, book or get in touch.
