About us

A neighbourhood pizza counter

Yonge Slice is a small counter shop that does one thing well: fresh pizza, fast.

Our dough rests for 48 hours, we grate our own mozzarella every morning, and every pie comes out of the oven in about eight minutes. Grab a slice on the way home or order a pie for the whole floor.
